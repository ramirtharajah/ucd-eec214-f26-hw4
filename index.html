Switched-Capacitor 2:1 Buck Converter

Consider the following difference equation for the output voltage VoutV_{out} of an NN:1 step-down series-parallel switched-capacitor voltage converter:

(CL+(N−1)Cfly+TSW/2RL)Vout[n]=(CL−Cfly)Vout[n−1]+CflyVin\begin{equation} \label{eq:SCdown} \left(C_{L} + (N-1)C_{fly} + \frac{T_{SW}/2}{R_{L}}\right) V_{out}[n] = (C_{L} - C_{fly})V_{out}[n-1] + C_{fly}V_{in} \end{equation}

where CLC_{L} is the load capacitance, CflyC_{fly} is the flying capacitance, RLR_{L} is the load resistance, VinV_{in} is the input voltage, and TSWT_{SW} is the switching period of the converter. Note that to derive Equation [eq:SCdown], it was assumed that the charge delivered to the load during the series-connected phase ultimately came from the input voltage source and that the change in output voltage did not affect the current drawn by the load. A 50% duty cycle was also assumed.
